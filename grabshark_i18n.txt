== Sync Workflow ==

[Local CLI]
└─ Tool: crowdin@4 (Homebrew tap: crowdin/crowdin) veya npm @crowdin/cli
└─ Upload Source: `crowdin upload sources`
└─ Upload Translations: `crowdin upload translations --auto-approve-imported --import-eq-suggestions`
└─ Download Translations: `crowdin download translations`

[GitHub Actions (Opsiyonel)]
└─ File: .github/workflows/crowdin.yml (henüz eklenmedi)
└─ Triggers: push (en/common.json) → upload sources, schedule (gece) → download → auto-PR
└─ Secrets: CROWDIN_PROJECT_ID, CROWDIN_PERSONAL_TOKEN

[Reorder After Download]
└─ Risk: Crowdin download key sırasını alfabetik bozabilir
└─ Fix: `tools/i18n/reorder.js` (henüz repo'da yok) — EN sırasını referans alıp 14 locale'i hizalayan tek scriptlik node işi.
